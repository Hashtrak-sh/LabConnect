Booking errors

Copy follows ERROR_HANDLING.md: what happened, why, what to do. The form keeps its data in every case.

SLOT_UNAVAILABLE
That slot was just takenSomeone booked the last place in Thu 26 Nov, 10 AM–12 PM IST. Pick another slot; everything else you entered is kept.
SITE_NOT_BOOKABLE
This lab can't take bookings right nowVasundhara Testing Laboratories paused new bookings at this site. Your answers are saved as a draft.
SCOPE_LINE_NOT_BOOKABLE
One test is not available"Carbon black content" is temporarily unavailable at this lab. Remove it to continue, or book it elsewhere.
OPEN_BOOKING_LIMIT
Too many open bookingsYour organisation has 20 bookings waiting for labs. Finish or withdraw one before sending a new request.
CONCURRENT_MODIFICATION
This booking changed while you were editingThe lab or someone in your organisation updated it. Refresh to see the latest; your edits are shown so you can apply them again.
SLOT_UNAVAILABLE in context
Only open slots are shown (OQ-69). Drop your sample within the slot.
