Print stylesheet: what prints, what hides

  • Prints: the labels only, two columns on A4, 10 mm margins, cut lines dashed.
  • Hides: header, navigation, the format panel and buttons (.noprint).
  • A label never splits across pages (break-inside: avoid); a new page starts after every 8 labels.
  • Thermal: one label per page, page size 50 × 25 mm, QR at least 18 mm.
